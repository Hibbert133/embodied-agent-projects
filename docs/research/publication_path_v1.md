## Evidence-based gap assessment

Current strengths: reproducible causal information boundaries, registered
experiments, negative results, diagnostic-cost evidence, and explicit evidence
about stochastic recovery. Missing: a demonstrated novel method, strong
task-relevant baselines, independent stream replication, broader task coverage,
and a complete paper with defensible scope.

The [2026 official CCF directory announcement](https://www.ccf.org.cn/Academic_Evaluation/By_category/)
specifies full/regular papers; workshop or demo papers do not satisfy this
publication target. Exact venue selection and deadlines remain to be verified
against the current official directory and calls when the contribution matures.

Recent change detection, nonstationary bandits, dual control, and robot

the proposed mechanism is new.

## First new causal experiment

The [crossed-context protocol](../protocols/evidence_persistence_crossed_development_v1.md)
holds source and target bias marginals fixed while changing their coupling.
It separates an evidence-persistence effect from generic task difficulty and
keeps the agent's evidence fixed before target outcomes. Its negative control
is a source-independent policy, whose coupling contrast must be zero.

The experiment includes all task states to preserve exact marginals. It is not
an operational failure-recovery evaluation. A positive result would justify
building and prospectively testing a detector of evidence validity. It would
not be an algorithmic contribution on its own; bias compensation working better
when the bias persists is expected from control reasoning.

The [completed one-shot experiment](../../reports/evidence_persistence_crossed_development_v1.md)
passed its frozen development gate: +41.80 percentage-point interaction,
95% task-cluster interval [38.67, 45.31]. It establishes a mechanism signal,
not a new algorithm. The next required contribution is a causally valid,
action-relevant evidence-validity and reacquisition method with strong baselines.

## Requirements before a submission-ready claim

1. **Contribution:** a precise new decision problem or method, distinguished
   from task-oriented exploration and online identification through a complete
   literature comparison and an explicit failure case for existing approaches.
2. **Method:** implement evidence-validity estimation and cost-aware reacquisition
   using only current and prior observations; demonstrate when it chooses to
   reuse, remeasure, or abstain. Establish either a useful analytical property
   under stated assumptions or a compelling mechanistic explanation.
3. **Baselines:** frozen/no-probe, always-probe, periodic refresh, recent-history
   identification, a standard change detector, and a task-oriented acquisition
   method with matched access and interaction budget. Oracle remains a labeled
   evaluator bound, never a training or agent input.
4. **Evaluation:** new preregistered independent chronological streams with fixed
   sample-size/power decisions, multiple task geometries and fault families,
   abrupt/gradual context change, and unchanged-context controls. Version any
   expansion beyond historical push-v3; never rewrite old frozen conditions.
5. **Ablations:** remove validity estimation, outcome uncertainty, and acquisition
   cost separately. Evaluate success, interaction cost, stale-evidence errors,
   detection delay, and confidence calibration at the independent-stream level.
6. **Reproducibility:** clean-commit manifests, seed separation, no future or
   cross-method information, uncertainty that respects dependence, and failure
   cases. CPU development may precede larger compute but cannot substitute for
   required replication.
7. **Paper:** full technical manuscript, figures, limitations, related work,
   artifact instructions, and verified target-venue fit. LLM integration is
   optional unless it supplies a measured capability unavailable to baselines.
