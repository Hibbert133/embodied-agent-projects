# ProbeMem Calibrated Verifier v2

Status: `CALIBRATION_FROZEN_BEFORE_EXECUTION`

## Question and immutable inputs

This development-only successor asks whether query-distance weighting, posterior
uncertainty, and explicit applicability constraints improve decisions about when
to override the frozen repeated-probe variance rule. It changes verifier
calibration only. The admission rule, eight-field signature, standardized RMS
distance, Beta(1,1) prior, top-k of ten, recent window of ten, registered probe,
two skills, environment, and deterministic threshold remain frozen.

ProbeMem Verifier Demo v1 code paths remain the unweighted baseline. Its config,
protocol, reports, failure audit, and output tree are immutable and cannot be
regenerated by v2 scripts. No GLM, validation, held-out execution, principle,
new task, feature, distance, or skill is authorized.

## Population and stages

Calibration scans seeds 4800--4899 in ascending order and stops at 40 Agent-
visibly eligible operational failures or exhaustion. Eligibility requires a
failed initial rollout and both registered candidates to be constructible before
either outcome is executed. It is incomplete below 30 operational or 12
evaluator-only exclusive-recovery cases. Exclusive outcomes never control
stopping.

Prospective development seeds 4900--5099 are blocked until calibration passes,
its thresholds and artifact hashes are committed, and a clean-tree manifest is
generated. Development stops at 60 operational cases or exhaustion and is
incomplete below 60 operational or 20 exclusive cases. It runs once without
replacement or extension. Seeds 5100--5299 remain reserved.

## Weighted posterior and applicability

For each action, chronological top-k records receive `w_i = 1 / (1 + d_i)`
using the existing normalized distance. Accepted adds `w_i` to alpha, rejected
adds `w_i` to beta, and inconclusive adds `0.5 w_i` to both. Weighted coverage
is `sum(w_i)` and effective sample size is
`sum(w_i)^2 / sum(w_i^2)`. Duplicate record IDs fail closed.

Every posterior records mean, variance, central 95% interval, nearest distance,
weighted coverage, ESS, weighted contradiction rate, and cited record IDs.
Alternative superiority uses 10,000 independently sampled Beta pairs with a
manifest-bound deterministic seed. True parameter interval coverage is not
identifiable from one Bernoulli realization and is reported as
`N/A_SINGLE_REALIZATION`; interval width and overlap are reported instead.

Both candidates must pass minimum ESS, maximum nearest distance, minimum
weighted coverage, and maximum weighted contradiction. Global and recent
weighted posterior preferences must be present and agree. Missing evidence,
ties, or conflict fail closed with all rejection reasons retained.

## Calibration selection

The executable calibration config freezes the complete 4,800-combination grid.
Each combination owns an independent chronological replay and appends only the
outcome of its selected action. A combination is eligible only with at least
four decisive overrides, override precision of at least 0.75, and at least two
more helpful than harmful overrides. Eligible combinations are ordered by
precision, net helpful overrides, accepted recovery, fewer harmful overrides,
fewer total overrides, then more conservative thresholds. No eligible
combination means calibration failure and blocks prospective development.

## Formal comparison and chronology

The methods are Frozen Deterministic, exact Unweighted Verifier v1, Weighted
Posterior with the exact v1 guard, Calibrated Selective Verifier v2, and an
Evaluator-only Oracle. Admission remains the exact v1 rule for every verifier.

All operational method decisions are serialized before paired candidate
outcomes are collected. Each method owns independent chronological memory and
appends only its selected outcome after fresh verification. Alternative outcomes
are evaluator-only. Oracle never retrieves or writes memory.

Calibration metrics use a common evaluator-only reference cohort built from the
Frozen Deterministic chronological memory so unweighted and weighted posterior
predictions see identical prior records and candidate outcomes. Operational
selected-action metrics remain method-native.

## Prospective promotion and stop rule

Integrity requires zero chronology, Oracle leakage, future-memory,
counterfactual-write, invalid-ID, and invalid-skill violations. Promotion also
requires helpful overrides to exceed harmful overrides by at least two,
selective override precision of at least 0.75, and pooled candidate Brier score
strictly below the unweighted reference verifier. It then requires either
accepted recovery at least two cases above Frozen Deterministic, or accepted
recovery no more than one below Frozen with at least 50 percent fewer harmful
overrides than Unweighted Verifier v1.

Failure is immutable and stops further Memory-verifier tuning, GLM, validation,
held-out, and principle work.
